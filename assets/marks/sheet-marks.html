SANSOS · Identity

The marks

One mark for every system in the registry. Each is its own lit niche — carved stone, a beaded brass band, and an opening with light behind it. The figure inside is read the way you read the horizon through a window: as a silhouette against the glow.

प्रथमम्The three given names

Named in purpose.md before the families existed, so they answer to no family. They take the square aperture — the plain trabeated opening that precedes the arch, and the shape of the maṇḍala everything else is set out on.

प्रथमाFamily I — centres of learning

Reserved for models, and the only family that gets the stepped śikhara, because these are the tallest things in the system. Doc 17 rule 5 keeps the family closed: two are spoken for, six names stand unspent.

द्वितीयम्Family II — what you inhabit

User-facing surfaces and privilege boundaries. These take the chaitya arch, because every system here is an enclosure and the arch is the enclosure that admits.

तृतीयम्Family III — what acts

Instruments and processes. The corbelled niche — the squared, lintelled recess a tool is set into rather than a space you enter. Each figure is open and has a direction.

रूपाणिFamily IV — surfaces and media

Where the system meets the senses, so these take the oculus: the round opening that faces outward and admits without framing. Akṣara is the bare letter skeleton and Varṇa is that skeleton carrying marks above and below, which is the shaping problem exactly; Nāda radiates where Rūpa bounds.

कालःFamily V — lifecycle

Systems that act on the system while it is running, so they take the cusped lotus arch — the one housing that is foliate rather than structural. Sañjīvanī is the only mark whose figure crosses its own outline: hot patching is defined by doing two incompatible things at once, cutting and not stopping.

35 marks against doc 17's registry. Six housings, one finial, one plinth, one beading rule — a new mark is an interior figure and nothing more, so the set extends without drifting, as long as the next one answers to a family.
