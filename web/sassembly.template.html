The same ELF, in a browser

Assembled by sadhana from Devanagari source, executed here by a RISC-V interpreter compiled to wasm. No third-party crate; no server. A store to 0x10000000 is a UART write on bare metal and a character on this page.
the surface it was granted
the machine’s own UART

Two of these are OS proofs: a machine boots at its reset vector in S-mode and writes to a device. atithi.sas is an application — a loader places it, a supervisor answers its two calls, and it never leaves U-mode. It cannot address a device at all, so its characters arrive on a surface it was handed and the machine’s UART stays empty. The two panes above are two different buffers, and which one fills is the whole difference between the two kinds of program.
